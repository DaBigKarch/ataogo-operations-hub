# Public research basis

Reviewed 4 October 2026. The scenario is an inference for demonstration purposes, not a statement of Ataogo requirements.

## Sources

   Public service headings include Asset Management, Offshore subsea intervention, Tank & Vessel cleaning, Marine vessel / Rig Mobilization, Instrumentation/control/electrical services, Risk Management Services (Safety, Reliability and Environment), Welding & Fabrication consultancy, Pipeline & flowline services, Engineering Procurement construction (EPC/EPCM), and Specialized Procurement & Logistic services. The company describes engineering/construction services for the energy sector and gives an Ekpan/Warri address.
2. Company LinkedIn page: https://ng.linkedin.com/company/ataogo-nigeria-limited
   Describes engineering and construction, project management, procurement, technical inspection, maintenance/repair and electrical/instrumentation services. Used as supporting company-published context, not proof of individual projects or internal systems.
3. NCDMB published January 2026 NCEC list: https://ncdmb.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/NCEC-Update-as-at-January-13-2026-1.pdf
   Contains entries for Ataogo Nigeria Limited in fabrication/construction, quality control inspection/testing and consultancy/manpower supply. This is a historical public listing. No present certification validity is asserted in this demo.

## How the research shaped the concept

A fictional flowline maintenance scenario connects integrity inspection, procurement of inspection kits, mechanical materials, inspection-unit readiness, field activity, safety actions and technical evidence. Other fictional projects illustrate offshore mechanical support, pipeline intervention and tank cleaning. These reflect service categories rather than copying actual projects.

No public evidence was used to claim Ataogo uses Excel, lacks ERP, has workflow failures or has approved these modules. No real client names, personnel, equipment, budgets, certificates, logos or project records are reproduced. The suggested modules and workflow are Vecora's illustrative design proposal.

Public source excerpts on the official site are incomplete; the interface therefore avoids detailed claims about specific technical methods or owned/leasable equipment. Equipment examples are fictional assets, not Ataogo's fleet. Service headings inform the concept, not operational instructions.
